# Sound demos

Factory presets at 140 BPM. Each demo plays **two bars of the kick alone**, then **the rumble comes
in on bar 3** for four bars, and the last kick rings out. The kick is the built-in Kick 01 G; the
presets are untouched, straight out of Blackveil, with no mixing or mastering.

Find them in the preset browser under the same category and name.
